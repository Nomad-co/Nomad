How it works

One place for context. Clear choices for access.

Nomad is designed to sit beside supported assistants, not replace their chat windows.

01

Save context once

Keep preferences and project decisions in a passport you can inspect and edit.

02

Grant a narrow read

Choose the assistant, fields, project, purpose, and expiry before access is allowed.

03

Review changes

Assistant-suggested updates wait for your approval. See activity in Access history.

Access horizon

Know what is shared, and when it ends.

Every grant has a scope and end date. Revoke it for future requests at any time. The example shows how that could read in the product.

Plain limits

Control the boundary Nomad owns.

Clear promises matter more than a magic-sounding sync claim.

Designed to do

  • Check grants before Nomad returns stored context.
  • Require review before a suggested change becomes a saved fact.
  • Show future access and activity in understandable terms.

Cannot promise

  • Force an assistant to call Nomad in every conversation.
  • Erase context already delivered to another service.
  • Automatically import or synchronize a vendor’s private memory.
