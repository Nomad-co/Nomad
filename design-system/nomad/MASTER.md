# Nomad interface direction — selection pending

The previous dark navy and green concept was rejected on 2026-09-16. Do not reuse that palette for new work. These three light directions are provisional; the user will choose one before application development.

## Shared product principles

- Make context access understandable before it is granted: show tool, exact fields, purpose, duration, and read/write scope.
- Let a person narrow or revoke access to their own fields, subject to organization and data-owner limits; never imply the person can share employer- or client-owned data outside that boundary.
- Use plain-language labels, clear focus states, keyboard-accessible controls, and WCAG AA contrast.
- Default to white or pale surfaces and neutral ink. Never default to dark mode or green accents.
- Mark illustrative content as sample data. Do not present a mockup as a functional product.

## Option A — Calm Passport

Warm white `#F8F6F2`, paper `#FFFFFF`, ink `#25262B`, secondary ink `#666872`, accent clay `#A25B43`. Editorial left navigation, a large context passport, and a compact access rail. Best for the individual employee's mental model of ownership and portability.

## Option B — Decision Inbox

Cool white `#F7F9FC`, paper `#FFFFFF`, ink `#1F2937`, secondary ink `#667085`, accent blue `#315FD4`, pending amber `#A76413`. Horizontal navigation, approval queue, and decision-detail pane. Best for fast review of access requests and consent changes.

## Option C — Policy Studio

White `#FFFFFF`, mist `#F5F5F8`, ink `#25252D`, secondary ink `#686874`, accent violet `#6657A8`. Admin command bar, policy matrix, and evidence strip. Best for IT/product buyers, with a clear distinction between organization policy and individual choice.

## Current status

Mockups only. No direction selected; no new production application code is authorized by the user's current request.
